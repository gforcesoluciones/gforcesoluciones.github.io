G Force Automations

Workflow automation for Google Workspace

G Force Automations is a private, single-user tool run by G Force Soluciones. It connects to the owner's own Google account to automate routine content and productivity work.

What the app does

  • Gmail: sends newsletters and drafts emails that the owner writes and approves.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides: creates, reads and updates the owner's own files (reports, trackers, study sheets, presentations).
  • Google Calendar: reads and creates the owner's own events.

Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. It only acts on the Google account of the person who signs in, and only when they run it.

Your data

The app does not sell, share or advertise with Google user data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
