G Force Automations

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how G Force Automations ("the app"), operated by G Force Soluciones ("we", "us"), handles information when you sign in with your Google account.

1. Information we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app may access only the data covered by the scopes you approve, which can include:

  • Your basic profile (name and email address) to identify your account.
  • Gmail: to send emails and drafts you create, and to read messages you ask the app to process.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides: to create, read and update files you work with through the app.
  • Google Calendar: to read and create events you ask the app to manage.

2. How we use information

Google user data is used only to provide the features you run in the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

3. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the device where the app runs and are never published. Data pulled from Google is processed on that device and kept only as long as needed to complete the task. We use reasonable safeguards to protect this information.

4. Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties, except when you direct the app to send content (for example, an email you choose to send) or when required by law.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reaching your data, and you can ask us to delete any locally stored data.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to G Force Soluciones through github.com/gforcesoluciones.
